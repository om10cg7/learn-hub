- 使い方: 写真をアップロードして ChatGPT の画像生成に渡す、写真→イラスト変換系プロンプト

## 要約

アップロードした写真を、手作りの層状ペーパーアート（ペーパークラフト風ジオラマ）イラストに変換する英語プロンプト。核は「写真の構図・視点・ランドマークは忠実に残したまま、質感だけを切り紙の重なりで再構成する」こと。指定のポイント:

- 焦点: 写真の中で最も重要なランドマークを1つ選び、中央の主役にする。副次的な要素は簡略化・削除
- スタイル: 手作りのレイヤード・ペーパーカット、立体ペーパーコラージュ／ペーパージオラマ。おとぎ話のような、古典的な絵本の雰囲気
- 紙の表現: 厚紙の層の重なり、切り口と紙の縁の見える化、前景・中景・背景を別々の紙レイヤーに分離。わずかに粗い厚紙の表面、繊維感、手切りシルエット
- 構図: 元写真の構図と遠近を保持しつつ、写実的な遠近法ではなく紙の重なりで奥行きを作る。装飾フレーム・境界はなし
- 色: 原色＋パステルの組み合わせ。コバルトブルー、コーラルレッド、暖色イエロー、ターコイズ、ピンク、クリーム、ミント、ラベンダー。写真の色は図式化したグラフィックカラーに置き換える
- 光: 写実的なライティング、光沢、反射、映画的効果、リアルな影はすべて禁止。奥行きは紙の層と重なりだけで表現
- 回避: フォトリアル、リアルな3Dレンダリング、CG感、写真テクスチャ、グラデーション、アニメ調、カートゥーン的誇張、無関係なオブジェクトの追加

## プロンプト（原文ママ、作者の返信8件を読み順に再構成して連結）

```text
Transform the uploaded photograph into a whimsical, colorful, three-dimensional layered paper-art illustration. The uploaded photograph is the PRIMARY SOURCE. First analyze the photograph and faithfully preserve its recognizable composition, viewpoint, major architecture, landmarks, skyline, waterfront, roads, vehicles, boats, landscape, and other important visual elements.

Select the single most important landmark or visual focal point from the photograph and make it the dominant central element. Simplify or remove secondary, distracting, or unnecessary details so the focal point becomes immediately recognizable and visually prominent.

STYLE:
- handcrafted layered paper-cut art
- dimensional paper collage / paper diorama
- whimsical and fairy-tale-like
- classical storybook atmosphere
- sophisticated vintage illustration
- charming, warm, playful visual language
- carefully constructed overlapping cardstock layers
- clearly visible paper edges and cut shapes
- multiple physical paper layers creating convincing depth
- foreground, middle ground, and background separated into distinct paper layers
- slightly raised paper pieces and visible overlaps
- tactile handmade cardstock / textured colored paper
- subtle paper grain and fibers
- crisp hand-cut silhouettes
- elegant simplified geometric forms
- minimal details but strong visual recognition
- cohesive design throughout the entire illustration

COMPOSITION:
- preserve the essential composition and perspective of the original photograph
- redesign the scene as a carefully composed paper diorama
- establish clear foreground, middle ground, and background layers
- make the main landmark visually dominant
- simplify unnecessary buildings and background details
- maintain recognizable relationships between major objects
- use overlapping paper shapes to create depth rather than realistic perspective
- no decorative frame or border
- slightly rough cardstock surfaces
- visible fibers and subtle irregularities
- layered sheets of paper with clearly separated edges
- tactile cut-paper construction
- dimensional relief created by stacked paper pieces
- the image should look physically assembled from many individual sheets of colored paper

STYLIZATION:
- simplify people, animals, vehicles, boats, buildings and other objects into one consistent, charming graphic language
- simple rounded geometric shapes
- elegant silhouettes
- no unnecessary surrounding ornaments

COLOR:
- vivid primary colors combined with soft pastel colors
- rich cobalt / deep blue, coral red, warm yellow, turquoise, pink, cream, mint and lavender
- harmonious but imaginative color relationships
- bright, cheerful and warm overall palette
- replace realistic photographic colors with stylized graphic colors while preserving the identity of the scene

PAPER MATERIAL:
- visibly textured handmade paper
- different paper textures for different layers

LIGHTING:
- no photorealistic lighting
- no glossy highlights
- no realistic reflections
- no cinematic lighting
- no realistic cast shadows
- depth should come primarily from the physical layering and overlap of paper
- only extremely subtle separation between paper layers when necessary to communicate thickness

AVOID:
- photorealism
- realistic 3D rendering
- CGI appearance
- painterly realism
- photographic textures
- excessive fine detail
- realistic shadows
- realistic highlights
- gradients
- glossy surfaces
- cinematic effects
- anime style
- cartoon exaggeration
- decorative frame or border
- adding unrelated objects

The final image should feel like a beautifully handcrafted, colorful paper diorama inspired directly by the original photograph: recognizable, sophisticated, whimsical, tactile, dimensional, and slightly nostalgic.
```
